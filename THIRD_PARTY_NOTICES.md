# Third-party notices

## cloudmusic-desktop-mcp

The experimental NetEase desktop adapter in `core/player.py` references the local Chromium control-channel and internal queue-action approach in https://github.com/Seraph310/cloudmusic-desktop-mcp. It is not an official NetEase API. Our adapter uses aiohttp rather than the project's MCP server. Internal module IDs and actions may change with client versions.
